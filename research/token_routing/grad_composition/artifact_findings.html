• Split before block 7–8; documents can leave by about block 24. Fine-tuning moves the gradient onto document tokens in the early-mid blocks: the query and answer tokens saturate first, and documents' share of ‖dL/dh7‖² goes 0.15 → 0.54 after only 31 mt4 steps. Past block ~8 that share falls with depth: dense 366 holds 0.57 at block 7, 0.24 at 16 and 0.11 at 24. A 10%-per-document keep ranked by the true gradient retains 0.96 of the parameter-gradient energy when it starts at block 7, against 0.85 for a random keep. Retention rises smoothly with L, so the training-time threshold at block 7 is about predicting importance from hL, not about where the gradient lives.
  • One keep decision is not enough; rescore once at ~16. Body-token rankings decorrelate with depth and with training. Spearman(G7, G24) goes 0.57 → 0.47 → 0.43 → 0.41 → 0.34 over base, 31, 61, 122 and 366 mt4 steps. A pyramid of 50% at block 3, 10% at 7 and 3% at 16 holds 0.70 of block 24's energy, vs 0.63 for a single 3% cut at block 7. On strmatch the gap is 0.60–0.67 vs 0.40–0.52. The 50% stage at block 3 is nearly free: it holds 0.92–0.96.
  • Gradient and activation targets disagree, and activation is no better than random. Spearman(Gb, ‖hb+1−hb‖) is 0.0–0.3 at every block. Forward ablation, keep 10% per document before block 7, ΔCE base → dense 366: gradient keep +0.13 → +0.23; activation keep ‖h7−h0‖ +0.47 → +0.50; random +0.35 → +0.66. On mt11 after 495 steps: gradient +0.25, activation +0.56, random +0.54. Answer-attention mass at the late full-attention blocks is close to the gradient on the base but degrades with training (+0.33 vs +0.23 at mt4 366; strmatch +0.92 vs +0.67).
  • Important-but-learned tokens are not the failure mode. Training does pull the gradient keep away from the attention keep: the share of the late-attention top 10% that the gradient keep misses rises from 0.37 to 0.56. But those tokens sit at the 64th–82nd percentile of G7, and keeping by attention instead is worse.
  • Blocks 0–6 hold 0.45 of the parameter gradient at the base, 0.20–0.40 after training. mt4: 0.45 → 0.31 → 0.28 → 0.25 → 0.23 (base, 31, 61, 122, 366); mt11: 0.45 → 0.39 → 0.39 → 0.34. The LM head grows from 0.02 to 0.11. Training blocks 0–6 only on full-context steps gives up a fifth to two fifths of the gradient energy, most of it early in training.
  • strmatch needs deep document processing at more than 10% per document. Its rows have 82 documents of ~25 tokens, so 10% per document is about 3 tokens each, mostly header. Its documents keep the most gradient deep in the stack: dense 0.24–0.29 at block 24 (nq 0.15–0.23), router-trained 0.39–0.45. Even the oracle keep costs ΔCE +0.59 to +0.67 after mt11 training (random +1.0 to +1.1). The router-trained model's strmatch CE stays 2–3× dense's (0.111 vs 0.034 at 495 steps), matching its eval deficit. Short-document tasks need a per-document token floor, or a larger budget for documents below ~50 tokens.
  • grouping's deficit is not in the loss. Router-trained and dense checkpoints reach the same teacher-forced CE on grouping (0.33 vs 0.33 at 495 steps). The oracle keep is free (ΔCE +0.00 to +0.01), and the block-7 keep holds 0.94–0.98 of block 16's energy. Whatever costs the router 0.05–0.07 on grouping shows up only in generation (long structured answers), not in the objective it trains on.
  • Merge rather than drop, at the span level. A 10% keep at block 7 drops 0.16–0.26 of G7², spread out: the top 10% of dropped tokens hold only 0.26–0.42 of it. Importance is smooth along a document (lag-1 correlation of log G7 0.70–0.86), so pooling runs of neighbours should lose little.
  • The router changes the model, and the LR explains part of that. At equal CE (mt4 rows, 0.115 vs 0.116), the router-trained checkpoint and the LR-matched dense one (LR 2e-6) differ as follows:
    • The router's forward has adapted to its own drop: a 10% keep at block 7 costs +0.02 vs +0.20. On mt11: +0.02–0.03 vs +0.25.
    • Its gradient is more early-heavy: blocks 0–6 hold 0.31 vs 0.22.
    • Its rankings are only slightly more stable across depth: the block-7 keep holds 0.85 vs 0.83 of block 16's energy. Most of the gap to dense at LR 5e-6 (0.74) is the lower learning rate, not the router.
