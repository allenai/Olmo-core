Keep vs length: does the best keep fall as rows get longer?

No. Best router keep is 20-40% at every length the proxy resolves (8k 40% tied with 20%; 16k 20-40% tie; 32k 40% beats the 20%/7% recipe at matched FLOPs on both seeds, 70% ties; 64k 20%, 40% slightly worse on both seeds). Low keeps break harder the longer the rows (10%/3.5% vs the recipe at matched FLOPs: +0.003 CE at 8k, +0.08 at 64k; 2.5% and 1.25% collapse at 32k/64k). Constant fraction fits; a constant kept-token count is rejected. 2k/4k saturate. Gold-aware keep grJ: 6-8x vs dense full FT on the mean but stalls on outlier; grJc (doc drop on contradiction only) is outlier-safe at 32k, not at 64k. Record: records/keep-fraction-vs-length.md.
