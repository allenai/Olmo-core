Three-Week GPU Audit

Where the 6,705 GPU-hours went across Beaker, Berkeley slurm and Lambda, how long jobs waited, what was lost, and the changes that would get more experiments through per week.

Verdict

  • The biggest loss is preempted Beaker training that restarts from step 0: 1,083 GPU-h (16% of all GPU time). Beaker preempts our jobs as soon as they pass their 1 h minRuntime because our workspace group ai2/oe-scaling is about 55% over its allocation target. Even low-priority jobs from other groups win. The research trainer saves no mid-run checkpoints by default, and 99% of our training runs finish within 4 h, so a minRuntime that covers the run would avoid most of this.
  • Waiting dominates turnaround on Beaker: 60% of job lifetime was spent pending. Jobs pinned to jupiter waited far longer than jobs that could also land on ceres. For 2-GPU jobs the median wait was 0.40 h vs 0.10 h, and the p90 was 3.0 h vs 0.8 h.
  • We left Berkeley headroom unused. Our preemptive_high use on jsteinhardt averaged 1.8 of the 8-GPU cap. In 72% of hours we had nothing running there, including 168 hours when ≥4 of our GPUs sat pending on Beaker.
  • Lambda is not idle. Other users held 20.5 of its 24 working GPUs on average, and our starts preempted 30 of their jobs. Without preempting anyone, Lambda had about 2.4 GPUs free on average, at every hour of the day.
  • Since about 10-19, 98% of our Beaker GPU-hours were allocated, because ai2/flex2 now sits in the oe-scaling group. Decided 2026-11-10: allocated is correct, and the "always unallocated" rule is retired.
  • Several feared problems turned out small. Unregistered jobs used 0.5% of GPU-h, insurance copies under 1%, and smokes plus probes 2%. Contention for the jsteinhardt cap happened in one hour (today).

Limits of the data

  • In-job GPU utilization is not measured. Nothing stores per-job SM utilization: ops stall samples are not kept, and wandb system metrics were not pulled. "Allocated but idle" is therefore unmeasured. The incident log names a few cases: a hung final save at 0% GPU, and empty-epoch livelocks of about 35 min × 3 jobs.
  • Beaker per-job timing covers experiments that are in the run registry or in the ops status cache (6,425 experiments, 7,553 attempts). Beaker's own report agrees with our total to within 1%, so little is missing. Listing every finalized jupiter job by author timed out.
  • Restart-from-scratch is inferred from the trainer default (no mid-run checkpoints unless --save-interval is passed; 47 of about 430 logged Beaker training commands passed it) and from the incident log. A preempted attempt's GPU-hours are counted as lost.
  • Duplicate detection matches run names across pools after stripping cluster tags and hashes. It misses copies that were renamed.
  • "Evals with no result row" matches on eval job id, run id or the parent run. Some experiments (PAIL sweeps, tokdrop in-training metrics) keep their numbers in their own logs, so the "training runs with no linked eval" count is mostly a registry-linking gap, not lost work.
  • Lambda capacity assumes 24 GPUs (the three nodes that ran jobs). jsteinhardt headroom assumes 50 non-RTX GPUs and counts only other users' preemptive_high jobs as blocking.
  • sacct retention covers the whole window on both clusters (records back to 2026-07-21). The Beaker report was readable with the org filter.
  • Session transcripts cover sessions on this machine's ~/.claude*/projects. Sessions run from other machines are missing.
